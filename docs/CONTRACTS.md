Routing recovery: k=20 active contacts plus at most k authenticated replacement candidates per bucket. Full buckets retain live incumbents; removal after two failed RPCs promotes the newest eligible cached contact. Replacements are previously authenticated hints, not fresh liveness proof or Sybil-resistant human identities. Refresh probes four globally least-recently active contacts within a two-second budget before iterative lookup. Successful authentication advances recency; fresh inbound activity prevents an older in-flight failure from counting. User/lifecycle cancellation does not count as failure. Canceled callers leave the bounded native queue promptly; their queued tasks retain quota slots until drained and cannot open sockets after cancellation. Blocked contacts are filtered from referrals/desktop contact views and replacement promotion.

Synchronization fairness: scan at most 16 signed records per invocation, with a default 2000ms budget (validated synchronizationMs override 1–10000). A budget stop advances past attempted records; completing a store sweep rotates the order of the three nearest replica candidates. This prevents a stable slow first record/closest replica from starving later records/reachable replicas in a stable fixture. User/lifecycle abort preserves the scan position. Dynamic churn/expiry can change numeric scan positions; subsequent sweeps revisit live records. No global convergence bound without usable transport and stable-enough retained records is promised.

Target-aware repair: each attempted record first resolves its target through iterative authenticated lookup, bounded to min(500ms, half the configured synchronization budget), within the overall cycle budget. Replica selection combines active contacts with freshly authenticated lookup results and chooses three nearest eligible peers. PUT likewise uses its lookup results even when live full buckets retained those peers only in replacement caches. Referral/lookup candidates may include bounded previously authenticated replacements; this does not promote them to active incumbents or establish fresh liveness. RoutingTable.stats reports active/replacement/bucket counts without exposing mutable state. The simulator drives production maintenance with a synthetic shared bootstrap and synthetic stream metadata; results cannot establish native Noise/NAT behavior.
