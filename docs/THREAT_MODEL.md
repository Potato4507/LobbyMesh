# Development threat boundaries

Dedicated media host has local-frame/URL-bound IPC and a separately scoped audio-only permission gate (default denied), bounded nonpersistent session reuse and fail-closed window teardown. VoiceCalls now links it to authenticated direct signaling and synchronous call teardown; no production voice UI activation exists. Native synthetic audio crosses two app runtimes through real Noise/authenticated SDP under one Electron main. Physical microphone/OS grants, independent full-client and hostile media-identity acceptance remain unverified. SDP is permitted only through dedicated media-frame IPC; regular desktop/overlay IPC excludes it. No formal security acceptance inferred.

Voice remains partial: local consent defaults off, strict bounded identity/call-bound offer/answer/ICE/hangup, explicit answer, abortable negotiation and locally observed connected-media lifetime. The controller delegates SDP/DTLS validation to the dedicated browser media engine; authenticated app control supplies descriptions. No address/SDP logging, recording or automatic incoming capture. Full independent-client binding review, physical permissions, glare/group/ICE restart and adversarial media acceptance remain. No working user voice controls or voice security acceptance claimed. See evidence/authenticated-call-media.md.

Untrusted peers can forge room claims, create Sybil keys, spam routing tables, replay records, withhold results, and exhaust resources. App keys prove possession only; they do not establish Roblox account, age or trusted room membership. DHT membership is not authorization. Direct peers can observe network addresses. Public infrastructure availability is external.

Implemented boundaries: versioned authenticated Noise RPC with app-key binding, quotas/deadlines and signed ephemeral records; narrow main-frame IPC; networking initially off; microphone permissions denied/no audio capture. Persistent local contact policy defaults deny independently of session/discovery. Windows desktop secrets use OS protection without plaintext fallback. Existing development loaders/tests remain explicitly separate. Node OpenSSL Ed25519; no custom cryptography.

Contact pairing: short-lived signed codes bind the author's app/transport keys, chosen name, nonce and expiry. Pure local preview grants nothing; explicit main-window approval must authenticate the pinned app identity before guarded atomic policy persistence. A copied code or one side's approval never changes the other side's permission. Identity/endpoint substitution, oversized/expired/malformed codes, blocked peers, stale preview tickets and canceled operations fail closed. Codes carry public app metadata, are transferable/reusable until expiry and are not a secret identity proof. A malicious person can substitute their own correctly signed code/name; users must compare fingerprints through a channel they trust. The app cannot establish a person's identity/age from the code. No grant action is exposed to the overlay or foreign windows. Newer revoke/block actions retain immediate denial while queued filesystem operations settle; canceled committed grants are compensated. Power-loss/crash durability during compensation remains unverified.

Unfinished: full assessment, Sybil-resistant admission, actual Roblox membership authorization, enforceable age/contact eligibility, media identity binding, durable replay/revocation, physical device/NAT acceptance, reporting/moderation operations and distribution review. Pairing is an explicit local app permission mechanism, not a child-safety or platform compliance approval. No reporting service or human moderation exists. Do not activate unsolicited contact based on mock or self-asserted membership, and do not describe the product as safety-accepted/release-ready.
